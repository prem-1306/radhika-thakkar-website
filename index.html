My Story

Radhika
Thakkar

Celebrity Bridal Makeup Artist & Mentor

My journey in the world of beauty began when I was just 12 years old, working as a mehendi artist. By the time I turned 15, I discovered my true passion for makeup and its incredible impact on a bride's special day.

Over the years, I've had the privilege of adorning more than 5,000 brides — each one a unique and unforgettable experience. But my work didn't stop there. I wanted to give back and inspire others. That's why I became a mentor, and today I'm proud to have trained over 200 students who are now thriving in their own beauty careers.

Today, my mission is bigger than ever — to empower 100,000 women to find their place in the makeup industry and turn their passion for beauty into a fulfilling, flourishing career.
